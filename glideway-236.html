The big guy has arrived

Glideway 236

Garage designed & worldwide tested

Colour chartWhere to buy

The wait is finally over… The big guy has arrived! This oversized creation is a true freestyler, just like the 176 — the same smooth and natural action, with a much larger profile.

Exceptionally stable and perfectly balanced, it settles naturally on the pause and performs flawlessly at any speed or retrieve style.

The key difference between the Glideway 236 and its smaller brothers is its impressive side slide. With the right input, the lure can glide sideways up to around 4 ft, creating a wide and irresistible presentation.

Lead Stickers · buoyancy

Depth control

Running depth ranges from the surface down to approximately 10–12 ft and can be adjusted using the Lead Stickers placed inside the lure's cavities. Out of the box, the Glideway 236 is floating. Filling the first cavity makes it slow floating or suspending; filling the second turns it into a slow sinking bait.

Buoyancy may vary slightly depending on water temperature and the material or thickness of your fishing line.
