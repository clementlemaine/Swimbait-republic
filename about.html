About

About us

Over 10 years of swimbait fishing, and a whole life dedicated to many other lure techniques.

We put all our experience into our lures to help anglers catch bigger fish in an easier way — with the highest quality raw materials and handcraft work, for the best possible price, while ensuring good margins for all our business partners.

01

How are our lures born?

First a concept or an idea clicks in our brain. Then comes the draw, after that a wooden prototype, and finally a lot of resin prototypes to try every possible weight configuration, bait thickness… This process is a mix of creativity and artisan skills.

02

Testing

One of the most important parts of the process: even if the lure swims great in the tank, we have to make sure it performs in different conditions and scenarios — and that fish love it!

2 yearsof field testing, at most, before a release

03

Production

Having an amazing bait means nothing to us if we can't make it available for everybody. We travel the world to find high quality factories to produce our creations, and we personally supervise the whole process — from shell injection to packing — to make sure everything reaches the quality level we aim for.
