Sneak Attack 711

Designed to adapt to multiple scenarios and techniques, with outstanding versatility across a 1 to 3 oz lure range.

Built with a Toray 11,000T carbon blank — extreme lightness, exceptional strength, and a weight-to-power ratio that's hard to match.

Pair it with a size 200 reel for perfect balance and handling comfort. Its smooth, progressive action allows precise control of lighter lures while keeping the power to snap cast a 3 oz lure over long distances with complete confidence.

Unlike typical swimbait rods, it absorbs the excess energy of the cast instead of sending it to your elbow and shoulder — so you can keep bombing big baits all day long without pain or fatigue.

The progressive action also absorbs the head shakes during the fight, significantly reducing the chances of losing the fish.

Designed to perform at 100% with braided line. Not recommended with fluorocarbon as the main line.

14titanium guides

Guide system

A guide layout engineered to channel line flow with maximum efficiency. The 14 titanium guides control every contact point and keep the line perfectly aligned, reducing friction and turbulence.

The short spacing between guides stops the line from forming loops or tangles during the cast or the retrieve, so wind knots are virtually eliminated. And the generous inner diameter lets leader knots pass with minimal friction, even with thick leaders.
