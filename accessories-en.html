Accessories

As the nerd anglers we are, we love to customise our swimbaits! Sinkers, Spare Tails and many others coming.

Accessory 01

Spare Tail

One more accessory to customise the look of your Glideway with a special colour touch. Some days, little details really make the difference — and this can be one of them.

Our tails are made of tough silicone: hard to deform and highly resistant to toothy fish.

Tip: remove the tail from the Glideway before storing it back in your tackle box, to avoid any possible deformation.

Accessory 02

Lead Stickers

Designed to adjust the buoyancy of your Glideway, so you can fish different layers of the water column — or simply compensate for the elements, like low water temperature or heavy wind.

To remove them, use the gap in the Glideway cavities to slide in the treble and pull the sticker out. Lead Stickers can also be used to fine-tune the weight of any other lure.

When should you add them?

  1. 01

    Suspend or sink

    When you want the Glideway to suspend or to sink — it's a floating bait by itself.

  2. 02

    Heavy wind

    When it's very windy: the wind pushes the line and tends to bring the lure up.

  3. 03

    High banks

    When fishing from a cliff or any structure high above the water, because you're pulling the bait towards the surface.

  4. 04

    Cold water

    When the water gets cold, to compensate the extra buoyancy — any lure floats more in cold water, which is denser than warm water.

  5. 05

    Max distance

    When you need maximum casting distance. Yes, even 3 extra grams will help you cast longer!
