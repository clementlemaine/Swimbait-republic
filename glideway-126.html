The little freestyler

Glideway 126

Garage designed & worldwide tested

Colour chartWhere to buy

If you believe that tiny glides should also have action (besides being cute), the Glideway 126 is your bait.

This small piece of art is a total freestyler like his big brother, but with an even more electric and reactive rhythm while still being very stable.

It has been perfectly balanced to adopt a natural position on the stop, but also to perform perfectly under any speed or type of retrieve.

Lead Stickers · buoyancy

Depth control

From surface to 10–12 ft, the depth of the Glideway 126 depends on the number of Lead Stickers you stick on the lure cavities. The lure is slow floating by itself; fill the first cavity and it turns into suspending. Fill the second cavity as well and it turns into slow sinking.

Rigging tip

Tie your line straight to the solid ring. For a better performance, don't add any snap or extra ring between the line and the solid ring. Finally, cast and enjoy!
